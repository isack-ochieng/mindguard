PRIVACY POLICY

MindGuard Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026 · Last updated: October 3, 2026

MindGuard is designed as a lightweight Android content-filtering application. This policy explains what information the app processes, where that information is kept, when information may be sent to third parties, and the choices available to you.

1. Who operates MindGuard

MindGuard is developed and maintained by Isack Ochieng. Privacy questions and requests can be submitted through the public MindGuard GitHub repository issue tracker.

2. Information MindGuard does not require

The current app does not require an account or registration and does not ask you to provide a name, email address, telephone number, profile, or payment information in order to use the core filtering features.

3. Network information processed on the device

When protection is active, Android routes relevant DNS traffic through MindGuard's local VPN interface so the app can make a domain-level filtering decision. The app compares requested domains with its local policy and can block a matching domain locally.

MindGuard's current architecture does not decrypt HTTPS payloads, inspect page contents, or operate a remote proxy for your general web traffic.

4. Information stored locally

MindGuard may store app settings on the device, including the configured not-trusted domain list and related protection preferences. The current app can also retain recent blocked-domain events for its local history interface.

Optional AI domain-intelligence results are cached locally so the app can continue using the most recent available snapshot.

5. Optional Gemini integration

MindGuard includes an optional domain-intelligence feature that can use Google's Gemini API when a valid API key is configured. The current implementation sends a predefined candidate-domain list and a classification prompt to Gemini.

The current implementation is specifically designed not to send your browsing history, visited-page contents, or the pages you read to Gemini. If you enable Gemini, the information sent to Google is subject to Google's applicable Privacy Policy and API terms.

6. Permissions and device capabilities

Depending on the features you use, Android may request access needed for:

  • a local VPN interface for DNS filtering;
  • internet and network-state access for normal networking;
  • foreground service operation while protection is active;
  • notification delivery for foreground-service status where required by Android; and
  • optional device-administrator protection and restart behaviour.

These permissions support the functions described by the app. MindGuard is not designed to collect contact lists, messages, photos, microphone recordings, or location history.

7. Sharing and disclosure

MindGuard does not operate a backend that receives or stores your browsing history for the core filtering feature. Information remains on the device unless you explicitly enable a feature that communicates with a third-party service, such as the optional Gemini integration described above.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid governmental request. No sale of personal information is part of the current MindGuard product model.

8. Security

MindGuard is designed to keep the filtering decision local and minimize unnecessary data transmission. Sensitive local values such as the configured clock secret are intended to be kept using Android-secure storage mechanisms provided by the app's current implementation.

No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure. Keep your device, operating system, and MindGuard installation up to date.

9. Retention and deletion

Local MindGuard settings and cached filtering data remain on the device until they are changed or removed, the relevant history is cleared, app data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled. Cached AI snapshots may be replaced when a later refresh succeeds.

Because the core application does not require a MindGuard account or maintain a central user database, there is no server-side MindGuard account to delete.

10. Children's privacy

MindGuard is a general-purpose device protection tool. It should be used by a parent, guardian, organization, or device owner in accordance with applicable law and with appropriate notice or authorization. MindGuard is not designed to collect personal profiles about children.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when MindGuard's features, integrations, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

12. Contact and questions

For privacy questions, corrections, or concerns about the handling of information, use the MindGuard GitHub issue tracker and identify the request as a privacy matter.
